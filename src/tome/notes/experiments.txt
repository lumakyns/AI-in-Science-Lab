CIFAR-100 ViT trained from scratch with 4x4 patches (8x8 grid), width 192, nine blocks, three heads, and r=4 merges per block: 65 tokens including CLS become 29. Balanced bipartite matching uses cosine similarity of head-averaged keys; CLS is protected. Token sizes weight merged values and enter attention as log-size bias. AdamW, five-epoch warmup and cosine decay run for 100 epochs, with online W&B project tome.

Fixed validation examples are traced at initialization and after every epoch. Each layer exports original-patch group IDs, with numbered/color overlays and compressed NPZ arrays in the run's images/patch_merges folder. IDs are local to each image/layer; equal IDs identify a shared surviving token. Source tracing is disabled for training. Validation selects the best checkpoint; the held-out test split is evaluated once at the end.
