CIFAR-100 ViT trained from scratch with 4x4 patches (8x8 grid), width 192, nine blocks, three heads, and r=4 merges per block: 65 tokens including CLS become 29. Balanced bipartite matching uses cosine similarity of head-averaged keys; CLS is protected. Token sizes weight merged values and enter attention as log-size bias. AdamW, five-epoch warmup and cosine decay run for 30 epochs, with online W&B project tome. The six-run sweep compares merging during training versus inference-only merging across matched seeds 0, 1, 2; both arms evaluate with r=4, and the inference-only arm trains with 65 tokens throughout.

The same first four real, unaugmented CIFAR-100 validation examples are traced at initialization and after every epoch in both arms. Each layer exports original-patch group IDs, with numbered/color overlays and compressed NPZ arrays saved locally in the run's images/patch_merges folder; resolved configs record sample indices and train/eval token counts. IDs are local to each image/layer; equal IDs identify a shared surviving token. Source tracing is disabled for training. W&B receives one update per epoch: train/validation loss and accuracy, learning rate, and epoch, plus the resolved config and best validation summary. Figures remain local; batch metrics, system statistics, console capture, and final test evaluation are disabled. Only train and validation splits are loaded; validation selects the best checkpoint.
